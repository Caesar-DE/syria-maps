## Tile providers
This project serves **its own** tiles rendered from its own data. Do not configure third-party tile
servers (e.g. tile.openstreetmap.org, commercial providers) in the `.osf` without written permission
and compliance with their usage policy. The OSM Foundation tile usage policy does not allow bulk or
app-default use.
