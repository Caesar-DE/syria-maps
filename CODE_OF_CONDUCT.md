# Code of Conduct / مدونة السلوك

This project serves people across Syria and the diaspora, regardless of region, religion, ethnicity,
language, gender, political view or background.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient; assume good intent. Arabic and English are both first-class languages.
- Discuss data and code, not people.
- Keep map content neutral and factual. Map what exists on the ground (OSM "on the ground" rule).

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, discriminatory or sectarian language.
- Publishing personal information about others.
- Using the project to mark people, groups or properties as targets, or to spread unverified safety claims.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers (see the repository's security/contact settings).
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban participants who
break these rules. Decisions are documented to the reporter.
